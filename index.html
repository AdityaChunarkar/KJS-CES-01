How Climate Intelligence Works

The platform follows four major stages to convert climate information into actionable heatwave warnings.

01

Monitor

Collect temperature, humidity and environmental information.

02

Analyse

Analyse current conditions and compare them with historical patterns.

03

Predict

Estimate the probability and severity of upcoming heatwave conditions.

04

Warn

Convert climate risk information into understandable warning levels.
